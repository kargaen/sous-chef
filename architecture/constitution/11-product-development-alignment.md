## Product-Development Alignment

The owner thinks in product behavior, user flows, and visible outcomes, and
refines from something working.

- Optimize for short feedback loops, visible progress, low-risk changes, easy rollback.
- Build layer-by-layer within the MVC split; prefer the smallest demonstrable step.
- MVP-first: no speculative extensibility, no completing later layers unprompted.
- When direction is uncertain, ship the smaller slice and ask.
